🚀

Apps and Folders

Open your programs, Store apps and folders with one click, each with its own icon

🌐

Websites

Keep your favourite sites on the desktop, shown with their own site icon

📝

Notes

Keep the text you use every day and copy it back with one click

🖼️

Pictures

See a picture large, copy it again or edit it in Paint

⌨️

Ctrl+V

Paste a picture, some text or a web link and it lands on the widget

🗂️

Tabs

Group your cards in tabs for work, home or anything else

🔝

Compact Icon

Shrink the widget to a small icon that stays on top, and open it with one click

🔒

Private

Everything stays on your PC; no account and no sign-in
