# Privacy Policy — My Everything Widget

Last updated: 2026-10-30

My Everything Widget does not collect, store or share personal data with the developer or anyone else.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you add stays on your PC, in the app's own data folder:

- the categories, app shortcuts, notes and images you add,
- the icons of your app shortcuts,
- window position, theme and always-on-top preference.

Uninstalling the app removes this folder.

## Network access

The app makes one kind of network request: when you add a shortcut to a website, it downloads that website's icon
(`/favicon.ico`) from that same website. No other server is contacted, and nothing about you or your other items is
sent.

## Other apps

The app lists the apps installed for your Windows account so you can pick one, and starts the programs, folders and
links you add when you click them. It does not read the content of those apps or files.

## Clipboard

The app reads the clipboard only when you press Ctrl+V in the widget, and writes to it only when you press a note's
Copy button.
